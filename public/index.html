What XyloSync leaves out

No accounts.

Open the link or type five digits. That’s the whole sign-up.

No app.

It’s a web page. Phones, laptops, tablets — anything with a browser.

No size limit.

Files stream straight into storage, from a one-line note to a 40 GB video.

Nothing kept.

Eight hours after a room is made, it deletes itself: files, chat and people.
